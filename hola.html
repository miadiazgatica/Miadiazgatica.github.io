Emanuela Orlandi was a 15-year-old "Vatican girl" who disappeared in Rome on June 22, 1983, becoming one of Italy's most notorious unsolved mysteries. As the daughter of a Vatican employee living within the city-state, her case has prompted over four decades of speculation, including theories of international terrorism, organized crime, and scandals within the Holy See.
